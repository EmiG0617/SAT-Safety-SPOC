# San Antonio District Safety SPOC

A mobile-responsive workspace for Safety Points of Contact to complete facility safety audits, preserve evidence and corrective-action context, locate SPOCs, review authorized history, and access controlled reference material.

## Roles

- Safety SPOCs create, save, validate, and submit audits.
- Supervisors and designated recipients review authorized submitted audits and deficiencies.
- Safety Office Administrators maintain inspection configuration and reference data.
- Read-only stakeholders review authorized submitted information.

## Experience

The interface uses a high-contrast TxDOT-appropriate blue and white system with clear required states, large touch controls, compact operational summaries, and a six-step audit workflow optimized for phones and tablets.

## Policy boundaries

Unresolved workflow, retention, draft access, submitted-edit, notification, and self-edit policy is labeled **Needs TxDOT Confirmation** and does not block confirmed audit functions.
